Privacy Policy

rclone-123 · home

rclone-123 is a personal app with a single user, its owner. It connects the rclone file-sync program on the owner's computer to the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

Through the Google Drive API, the app can read, create, change and delete files and folders in the owner's Google Drive, along with their names, sizes and dates. It asks for nothing else from the Google account.

How that data is used

  • Only to copy and arrange files between the owner's computer and the owner's Drive, when the owner runs rclone.
  • Files travel directly between that computer and Google. The app has no server of its own, and no copy is kept anywhere else.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any data with anyone.
  • It does not use any data for advertising, analytics or profiling.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking of any kind.

Google API Services User Data Policy

rclone-123's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page.
